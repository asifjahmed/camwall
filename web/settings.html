Camera sources

    
Where camwall's cameras come from: state transportation departments, cities, toll roads and others. Each source's
      list is fetched again daily on this computer for the sources you use. Some ask apps like this one not to use
      their cameras, or need a key of your own; those are off until you turn them on.

About and privacy

    

      
camwall runs only on this computer. It has no account and sends nothing about you anywhere:
        it fetches camera lists and pictures straight from the agencies that publish them, and map tiles from
        OpenStreetMap or the provider set above. Your settings and walls are kept in .

      
Camera pictures belong to the agencies that publish them; see each source's
        site and terms above. Where an agency asks for credit or a disclaimer, it's shown with its cameras and its source. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Town and ZIP code locations: US Census Bureau.
